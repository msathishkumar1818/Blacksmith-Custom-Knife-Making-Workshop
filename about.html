Metallurgical Alchemy

The Science of Fire & Quenching

Achieving 62 HRC edge retention without catastrophic brittleness requires precise thermal control down to the single degree.

THERMAL CYCLE I

Differential Clay Quenching

Refractory clay applied to the spine slows cooling during oil quench, creating a flexible 48 HRC spine and a glass-hard 63 HRC edge separated by a visible Hamon line.

THERMAL CYCLE II

Liquid Nitrogen Cryogenic Bath

Submersion at -320°F transforms soft retained austenite into ultra-dense martensite crystals, eliminating micro-chipping along the razor apex.

THERMAL CYCLE III

Triple Dual-Stage Tempering

Three separate 2-hour heating cycles relieve internal lattice stress, ensuring the finished blade can flex 45 degrees without taking a set.
