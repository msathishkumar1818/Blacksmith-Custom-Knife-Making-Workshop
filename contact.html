Forge Visitor Guidelines & Safety

1
Footwear & Attire All visitors stepping into the active forge floor must wear natural fiber clothing (cotton/wool/leather) and closed-toe leather boots. Synthetic materials are strictly prohibited near coal hearths.
2
Eye & Hearing Protection Industrial safety goggles and acoustic ear muffs are provided at the entrance and must be worn whenever power hammers or grinders are active.
3
Children & Pets Due to extreme temperatures and sharp grinding machinery, children under 16 and pets are not permitted on the forge floor without prior administrative clearance.
