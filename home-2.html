Micro-Engineering & Ergonomics

Anatomy of an Heirloom Blade

Every millimeter of spine curvature, choil beveling, and handle weighting is calculated to become an invisible extension of the palm.

1

Full Tapered Skeleton Tang

The steel billet runs the entire length of the grip and tapers towards the pommel to position the center of gravity precisely 5mm ahead of the bolster.

2

Chamfered & Polished Choil

The underbelly of the blade is hand-radiused to 400-grit satin, preventing hot spots and blisters during high-volume culinary pinch grips.

3

Solid Brass Mosaic Pins

High-tensile brass and copper mosaic tubes mechanically lock the stabilized wood scales to the tang alongside marine-grade epoxy.

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

How long does a bespoke custom blade commission take?

Depending on Damascus layer complexity, rare handle sourcing, and current queue depth, lead times typically range between 4 to 8 weeks.

How do I maintain and care for high-carbon Damascus cutlery?

Hand-wash only, dry immediately, and apply our organic camellia oil after use. High-carbon blades develop a protective heirloom patina over decades of service.

Can I provide my own sentimental handle materials or timber?

Yes. We frequently stabilize family heirloom timber, antler, and historic artifacts in our vacuum resin chamber for custom handle integration.
